# Harbour Cafe

## Opening hours

Monday to Friday: 7am to 3pm. Saturday: 8am to 2pm. Sunday: closed.
Public holidays: we may close or open shorter hours, so please call us to check.
The kitchen stops taking food orders 30 minutes before closing.

## Menu

Breakfast is served until 11am: eggs benedict $24, big breakfast $26, granola bowl $16, avocado toast $19.
Lunch is served from 11am: chicken burger $23, salmon salad $25, beef nachos $22, soup of the day $15.
Kids' menu: pancakes $12, toastie $9, fluffy (frothed milk) $4.
Cabinet food (muffins, scones, slices) is available all day.

## Coffee and drinks

Coffee: flat white, latte, cappuccino, long black and mocha. Small $5, large $6.
Other drinks: hot chocolate $5.50, chai latte $6, loose-leaf tea $5, fresh orange juice $7, smoothies $9.
Oat, almond and soy milk cost an extra $1.

## Dietary options

We have gluten-free bread (+$2), and vegetarian and vegan dishes are marked on the menu.
We are not an allergen-free kitchen. If you have an allergy, please call us before you come.

## Kids and dogs

Kids are welcome. We have high chairs and a small play corner.
Dogs are welcome at the outdoor tables, and we have water bowls outside.

## Location and parking

We are on the waterfront at Harbour Road, Auckland.
There is free street parking for 2 hours, and the beach car park is a 2-minute walk away.
The cafe is wheelchair accessible.

## Payment and Wi-Fi

We take EFTPOS, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We don't accept cash.
Free Wi-Fi is available. Ask the staff for the password.

## Bookings and groups

You can book a table for 1 to 8 people through our chat assistant.
For groups of more than 8, or to book the whole cafe for an event, please call us.
We hold a booked table for 15 minutes.
